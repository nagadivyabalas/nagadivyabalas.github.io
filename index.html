01 · ABOUT

About me

I am a Doctoral Researcher in Computer Science at Aalto University, Finland. My research focuses on requirements engineering and explainable AI, with particular interest in how explainability can be systematically defined and incorporated into AI system development.

My doctoral research explores how requirements engineering can support the development of transparent, trustworthy, and accountable AI systems in the context of evolving regulatory requirements. My work combines research in software engineering, requirements engineering, and responsible AI to develop practical approaches for organizations developing AI systems.

02 · PhD THESIS

The role of requirements engineering in defining the explainability of AI systems

My doctoral research examines how requirements engineering can support the definition of explainability in AI systems, addressing the growing need for transparent and trustworthy AI amid evolving regulations such as the EU AI Act.

My work explores how organizations define ethical guidelines, how practitioners perceive explainability, and how explainability can be systematically incorporated into AI system development.

The research contributes practical tools, including a model of explainability components and a lightweight approach for regulatory compliance, to help organizations develop more transparent and accountable AI systems.
